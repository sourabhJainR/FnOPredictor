How the score works

The confidence score is a heuristic ranking score, not a calibrated probability of profit. It combines directional bias from put/call open interest, option liquidity, spread cost and implied volatility. It is not a backtested win rate.

Payoff estimates use executable-side quotes where available (ask to buy, bid to sell). If only LTP is available, the result is marked indicative. Brokerage, taxes, slippage and partial fills are excluded.

For research only. No order placement. Options can lose the full debit paid; review liquidity and contract specifications before trading.
