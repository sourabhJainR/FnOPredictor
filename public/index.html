How the application learns

Every new provider snapshot records the best call and put spread. When the paper position closes, the realized simulated outcome is added to the ledger. After 20 closed outcomes for a side, its observed win rate adjusts that side's ranking score with a small bounded correction. This is outcome calibration, not proof of predictive skill; small samples remain labelled insufficient.

Profit is booked at a 25% gain on spread debit, loss is cut at a 30% debit decline, or the position is closed at 15:20 IST. If exit quotes are unavailable, the position remains open and is not assigned a made-up result. Daily reports show realized paper P&L, trades and recorded predictions.

Local research application. No order execution. Paper fills are estimates and exclude brokerage, taxes, slippage, queue priority and partial fills. A provider must return the nearest weekly expiry with executable bid/ask quotes.
